    How it works

    1. Connect TikTok and Instagram once (left side). Log in, then close that window.
    2. Describe the videos you want, in plain words. Add must-have hashtags if you like.
    3. Press Start hunt. The AI plans searches, the agents scroll, and every video is judged against your description. Matches land in your folder with a log of why each was picked.
