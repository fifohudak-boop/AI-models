    How it works

    1. Connect TikTok, Instagram and Pinterest once (left side). Log in, then close that window.
    2. Drop a reference video — one that looks like what you want — or describe the videos in plain words. Both together work best.
    3. Press Start hunt. The agents look at lots of videos, every one is scored on how much it looks like your reference, and the best ones are saved — as many as you asked for, as MP4s that play on any Mac.
    4. Found one that's spot on? Press More like this on it to use it as a reference next time.
